# CK bench triage, 2026-11-17

Desk triage of the 47 record types the CK bench of the night of 2026-11-17 left
`inconclusive` (`docs/ck-observations.md`, sittings `ck-20261117-11` to `-18` and `-21` to
`-25`; pairs under `C:\Modding\kitkraft-observe\ck-2026-11-17\bench-*\`). No CK, game or MO2
was run. Every difference of every pair was diffed with KitKraft's own tools (the oracle
reports' `kit diff` items, `kit inspect --plugin` on both files for the payload bytes, and a
read of `Starfield.esm` for the corpus counts quoted below) and put in exactly one class:

- **A**: KitKraft wrote something wrong or non-canonical that the CK corrected. **A fixed**:
  the writer or a validate rule changed tonight, with a byte-expectation test. **A open**:
  the fix needs the schema (another lane's tonight) and is a `docs/gaps.md` row.
- **B**: the CK normalises bytes that carry no meaning. Explained by a narrow rule of
  `explained-differences.toml`: one subrecord, one exact transformation that
  `cargo xtask oracle ck --rules` checks on the payloads (`Transform` in
  `xtask/src/ckoracle.rs`). **Pending the owner's review of the rules**; no
  `docs/schema/evidence.toml` entry was changed.
- **C**: a consequence of how the recipe built the record; the recipe is fixed for the next
  sitting, no oracle change.
- **D**: unexplained; the last column says what the next sitting should isolate.

"Clone" is the type's `[[clone]]` of a vanilla record, "new" its EditorID-only `[[new]]`
record (`types/<TYPE>.toml`). Commits are on branch `opus/cktriage-20261117`.
