# Foothold gap matrix (M5-accurate)

Reconciles the owner's Foothold capability inventory (`docs/foothold/engineering-surface.md`,
sections A to I and K) with what `main` proves, as the owner's 2026-10-15 decisions ask
(priority F, "Deliverable"). Established on `main` at `fcb443a` on 2026-10-15; checked at
`5d03d01` on 2026-10-16 (M5.F2 and M5.F4, lane F), partly refreshed by lane D round 4,
checked as a whole at `81272e2` on 2026-10-16 and again at `630b3be` on 2026-10-31 (after
the first local-agent campaign, ADR 0014).

**Generated and hand-checked parts.** One block is generated: the headline between the
`parity:headline` markers ("Headline", below), which every `cargo xtask parity` run
rewrites from the corpus, the schema and `docs/schema/evidence.toml` of that moment. It
names those inputs, and it is never edited by hand. Everything else here is
hand-maintained and was checked at the dates it gives: the record-family and capability
rows, their evidence cells, the row tally under the headline and the notes.
`crates/schema/tests/reconcile.rs` (in `cargo xtask ci`) checks the headline against
`docs/record-parity.md` and the corpus, and each record-family row's types and
Typed-readable against the current schema and `docs/record-parity.md`.

**The hand-maintained rows were last checked as a whole at `630b3be` on 2026-10-31**
(the M5.F2 method, after the first unattended local-agent campaign of 2026-10-26 to
2026-10-30: 60 merged tickets, `plan/STATUS.md` "Campaign", `plan/tickets/README.md`
"Queue"; the previous whole check was `81272e2` on 2026-10-16, evening). A cell changed
since then names the commit or ticket that changed it. The check used a release `kit.exe`
built from `630b3be` (SHA-256
`18f63c02b5c0ba27182ab97633bed98fabff72d5d630000a29880f00ef95bf4b`; `kit --version`

## Statuses

Six statuses, never collapsed into one (decisions, "Acceptance criteria"; AGENTS.md
"Reporting"). Schema presence is not feature support.

| Column | Meaning | Values |
| ------ | ------- | ------ |
| Parsed | Every corpus record of the type is read and written back byte-identically (unedited) | yes, no |
| Typed-readable | The schema types its members at `corpus` confidence and every corpus occurrence decodes, with 0 re-encode conflicts | yes; partial (a member is below `corpus` confidence, `reference` or `container-reference`, counted as `kit schema --type <TYPE>` prints it; or a payload the feature needs is opaque or unknown); no (inventory or lstring-only definition, or none) |
| Typed-writable | A KitKraft write of the type (`set`, `new`, `clone`, `place`, `build`) is covered by a test or a built, validated, roundtripped recipe or prototype | yes (a test writes every typed member, or the type's write is the recipe's subject); partial (a recipe or prototype writes some members); untested (the generic path accepts it, nothing proves it); no (refused, or members opaque) |
| CK-diff | A CK-authored or CK-re-exported fixture compared against KitKraft (`docs/ck-observations.md`) | pass, fail, not-run, inconclusive |
| Game | Observed in the running game on `KitKraft Dev` and recorded in `docs/game-observations.md` | pass, fail, not-run, inconclusive. An owner result counts once a ledger entry holds it with the plugins' hashes (M5.F4: `game-20261015-02` and `-03`, grade "owner report"); a result whose hashes cannot be recovered is `inconclusive` |
| Save/reload | The game observation survives save, quit and reload (persistence protocol steps 1 to 4) | pass, fail, not-run, inconclusive |

`n/a`: the status does not apply to that row.

MX measures typed-readable its own way: `pass` when every occurrence of the type decodes
and no signature is unknown, with the typed share of payload bytes beside it. That is not
this matrix's Typed-readable (which also asks for `corpus` confidence), so each row quotes
MX's result in its evidence cell and keeps its own column.

  type and `set` any typed member, but a member below `corpus` confidence is refused unless
